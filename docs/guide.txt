@node what "What it is"
A dock along one edge of your Workbench screen.

Click an icon to start its program; drag icons on from Workbench to add
them. The dock shows which programs are running, brings their windows to
the front, and can hide itself until you need it.

-- Next: {What you need|need}
@end

@node need "What you need"
== Minimum
  AmigaOS 3.2, or 3.1.4, 3.5, 3.9, or a 3.1-based system that has
  workbench.library and icon.library 44 or newer.
  A 68030 or better, with Fast RAM. A stock 68020 A1200 works, slowly.

== Best
  A 68040 or 68060, or a PiStorm, and a graphics card with a 16- or
  24-bit screen (Picasso96). AGA screens work, more slowly.

-- Without ReAction's chooser.gadget (before 3.2) the lists in the
-- settings window are cycle gadgets: a click steps to the next choice.
@end

@node install "Installing"
== Install and start
Put the SimpleDock drawer anywhere and double-click *SimpleDock*. There
is no installer. Double-click *SimpleDock.guide* to read this manual in
MultiView.

The first start creates *ENVARC:SimpleDock.config* and fills the dock
with the standard icons it finds: the Workbench disk, Shell, MultiView,
Clock, the Prefs, Tools and Utilities drawers, and Ram Disk. It adds as
many as fit.

== Start with Workbench
Copy the *SimpleDock program and its icon* to *SYS:WBStartup*.

== Remove SimpleDock
Quit SimpleDock, then delete its drawer and ENVARC:SimpleDock.config. If
you put a copy in WBStartup, delete that program and its icon too.
@end

@node five "Five essentials"
== 1. Click an icon
Start a program, or open a drawer, disk or document. If the program is
already running, its windows come to the front instead.

== 2. Drag an icon onto the dock
Drag it from Workbench to a gap between two dock icons to add it there.

== 3. Drag an icon off the dock
Remove it from the dock. The file on disk is kept.

== 4. Drag an icon along the dock
Move it to a new position; the other icons make way.

== 5. Show the dock with the hotkey
Press *Ctrl+Alt+Shift* together, then release them. This reveals the
hidden dock or brings it to the front. With auto-hide on, the same
hotkey hides the dock when it is already visible.

Changes to the dock's icons are saved as you make them.

Open *Dock > Settings...* in the Workbench menu bar to change its
appearance and behaviour: see {Basic settings|simple}. The Dock menu also
contains About and Quit.

-- These five actions are enough to get started. Read on when you need
-- more detail.
@end

@node running "Running programs"
A *dot* under an icon means its program is running; two copies or more
show a count on the icon.

A program that is *not on the dock* shows up too, at the end behind a
thin line, with its own icon, for as long as it runs. Open Shells are
grouped under one Shell icon.

  *Click*              all the program's windows to the front; click
                     again to go through them one at a time
  *Shift+click*        another copy of the program
  *Del*                with the pointer over the icon: closes the program
                     (its windows are asked to close, as their close
                     gadget would; a program may ask you first)

To *keep* a running program on the dock, drag its icon in front of the
line. To *never see* one there, drag it off: the dock asks, then leaves
it out. To show it again, select it in *Hidden programs* in the settings
window and click *Remove* ({Programs|programs}).

While Workbench has drawer windows open, a *Workbench icon* sits right
after the line and counts them; a click brings them all to the front. A
docked drawer that is open gets a dot of its own.

-- See also: {Long press|longpress}, {Force Quit|forcequit}
@end

@node longpress "Long press"
Hold the left mouse button on a running program's icon until a list of
its windows opens. Let go over a window to bring it to the front. Let go
anywhere else and the list stays open:

  *click a window*         brings it to the front
  *point, press Del*       closes that window
  *below the line*         what can be done with the program:

    *Keep in Dock* / *Remove from Dock*
    *Show in Workbench*      opens its drawer
    *Minimise*               hides its windows; the dot pulses.
                           *Show (n)* brings them back - so does a
                           click on the icon.
    *Close*                  closes it
    *Force Quit...*          appears after Close fails:
                           see {Force Quit|forcequit}

  *Esc*, or a click elsewhere, closes the list.

With several windows, Minimise, Show and Close end in "..." and the list
asks which one, or All.

-- To drag the icon instead of opening the list, keep the button down
-- and slide along the dock.
@end

@node autohide "Auto-hide"
With *Auto-hide* on in the settings, the dock hides when the pointer
has moved an icon's size away from it. Move the pointer to that screen
edge to reveal it again.

The *hotkey* (Ctrl+Alt+Shift) shows it and keeps it shown until the
pointer has visited it and left; pressed while the dock is shown, it
hides it. *Ctrl+Alt+H* switches auto-hide on or off
({Keyboard shortcuts|keys}).

-- With auto-hide, icons cannot be dropped onto the hidden dock, because
-- Workbench locks the screen during a drag. Press the hotkey first.
@end

@node drops "Dropping files"
Drag icons from Workbench:

  *onto a program icon*       they are opened with it - a text file onto
                            your editor
  *onto a drawer or disk*     they are copied into it, with their icons,
                            dates and comments. A file already there:
                            Replace or Skip. Drawers are never merged.
  *between two icons*         they join the dock
  *into a Shell window*       their paths are typed ({Shell drops|shelldrops})

A *beep* means a drop was refused: a drawer into itself, a whole disk,
the Trash, or a full dock.

-- "Drop on a drawer" in the settings ({Programs|programs}) can make
-- drops move the files instead, when they are on the same disk.
@end

@node missing "Missing icons"
An icon is dimmed and marked *?* when its file or disk is not there.
Click it to be asked whether to remove it.

The dock never removes it by itself: a disk may just not be inserted.
@end

@node keys "Keyboard shortcuts"
== Show the dock: Ctrl+Alt+Shift
Press together, then release: see {Auto-hide|autohide} for how this
hotkey behaves when auto-hide is on. Another program using the same
keys (Directory Opus does): change it under Keys ({Shell drops and
Keys|advshell}) - other modifiers, a key, or the middle mouse button
(MBUTTON, or CTRL MBUTTON and the like).

== Minimise a window: Ctrl+Alt+M
Minimise the active window, just like Minimise in the long-press list.

== Minimise a group: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M
Which windows are minimised depends on what is active:

  Drawer window        all Workbench drawer windows
  Program window       all windows of that program, across every copy
  Shell window         all Shell windows
  Workbench backdrop   every window
  No active window     every window

When no windows are left showing, press the same shortcut to restore
them all.

== Toggle auto-hide: Ctrl+Alt+H
Switch auto-hide on or off. The choice is saved in the settings.

Change these combinations under Keys ({Shell drops and Keys|advshell}).
Enter *NONE* to disable a shortcut.

-- While SimpleDock is running, these combinations are reserved for it.
-- Other programs will not receive them.
@end

@node shelldrops "Shell drops"
Drag icons from Workbench into a Shell window to *type their paths* at
the cursor. Names containing spaces are put in quotes:

    "Ram Disk:" Work:Music

Nothing is run; add to the line or press Return yourself.

Drop them *onto the dock's Shell icon* to send their paths to the
frontmost Shell window and bring it to the front. If no Shell is open,
SimpleDock starts one and types the paths there.

A single *archive* gets the command that unpacks it, if its tool is in
C: or in Workbench's path:

    lha x "Work:Downloads/Game.lha"

    .lha .lzh   lha       .zip        unzip      .tar     tar
    .lzx        lzx       .tgz .tar.gz tar       .gz      gzip
    .7z         7z        .rar        unrar      .bz2 .xz bzip2, xz

Choose the destination with *Unpack archives to*
({Shell drops and Keys|advshell}): the Shell's current drawer, RAM:, or
a drawer of your choice.

-- Works in the standard console and in others such as KingCON. OS 3.2's
-- own Shell types paths itself, so there the dock leaves Shell windows
-- alone. A console that types paths itself would show them twice: set
-- "Icons into a Shell" to Off.
@end

@node forcequit "Force Quit"
A program that ignores Close keeps its long-press list; the second time
you open it, the list offers *Force Quit...* It works in two steps:

  1. SimpleDock sends the *break signals* (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+E and
     Ctrl+F), giving the program a chance to stop and clean up.

  2. If it is still running after two seconds, a requester asks for
     confirmation. *End it* removes
     the program's tasks (every copy, also one without a window) and
     closes its windows and its own screen.

Step 2 is the same as Scout's Remove, with the same risks: AmigaOS
cannot reliably reclaim everything the program holds. Memory, files and
locks can remain in use until a reboot. Ending a program inside a system
call can crash the machine. *Save your work first.*

-- A Shell program ended this way no longer appears in Status.
@end

@node simple "Basic settings"
*Dock* menu > *Settings...* (on 3.5: Tools > SimpleDock Settings...).
Changes show on the dock at once and are saved when you close the
window. The dock stays shown while the window is open.

== Place
  Position         Top, Bottom, Left or Right
  Gap to edge      space between the dock and the screen edge
  Auto-hide        off, or how fast the dock hides
  In front         keep the dock in front of other windows
  Hotkey           the keys that show the dock: CTRL ALT SHIFT; or the
                   middle mouse button: MBUTTON, CTRL MBUTTON, ALT
                   SHIFT MBUTTON... (HOTKEY)
                   (modifiers only: pressed together, let go), or a
                   combination with a key, such as CTRL ALT D

== Icons
  Icon size        own size, or shrunk to Small / Medium / Large
  Icon spacing     space between the icons
  Text             labels below the icons, on hover, off, or labels
                   only (no icons)

== Look
  Colour           six colours, or *Custom...* for your own (a picker
                   with red, green and blue sliders)
  Transparent      Off: a solid dock. On: the screen shows through,
                   tinted with the colour
  Opacity          how dark the transparent dock is
  Corners          square to round

Click *Advanced >>* to show the remaining settings alongside the basic
ones. On a small screen, they replace the basic settings:
{Fine-tune|finetune}, {Programs|programs},
{Shell drops and Keys|advshell}, {System|system}.

-- The keyword each setting has in the settings file is given in
-- parentheses on those pages; you only need it for {Keywords|keywords}.
@end

@node finetune "Fine-tune"
  Alignment        Centre, Start or End along the edge (POS)
  Offset           shift along the edge, pixels (OFFSET)
  Gap to edge      pixels (DISTANCE)
  Icon spacing     pixels (ICONDIST)
  Icon size        shrink icons bigger than this many pixels; 0 = own
                   size (SCALEICONW, SCALEICONH)
  Most icons       at most this many; 0 = as many as fit (LIMIT)
  Corner radius    pixels, 0-32 (RADIUS)
  Opacity          0-100 (OPACITY)
  Auto-hide speed  0 = off, 1 = instant; otherwise n x 70 ms per slide
                   (AUTOHIDE)
  Colour           #rrggbb (TINT)

-- Basic settings and this one stay in step: Corners "Large" sets the
-- radius to 18; a radius with no preset shows as "Other" there.
@end

@node programs "Programs"
  Running programs Off, Dot, or Dot and number (RUNNING)
  Click when       Bring to front, or New copy - then Shift+click brings
  running          to the front instead (CLICKNEW)
  Start programs   SimpleDock (it then knows at once when a program
  with             ends), or Workbench, for a program that does not
                   like being started by the dock (WBSTART)
  Shell icons      Start at once, or Ask first: an "Execute a file"
                   requester with the command, as a double-click gives
                   (SHELLASK) - see {Shell icons and projects|shellicons}
  Drop on a drawer Copy, or Like Workbench: moved when on the same disk
                   (DROPMOVE)
  Long press       Normal, Long or Longer: 0.3 / 0.4 / 0.5 s (HOLDTIME)
  Programs not on  Show or Hide the programs that are not on the dock
  the dock         (RUNHIDE)
  Workbench icon   Show or Hide the icon for the open drawer windows
                   (WBHIDE)
  Hidden programs  the programs you dragged off the dock, by name; pick
                   one and *Remove* to see it again (IGNORE)
  Other docks      the other docks running (main dock only): Settings...
                   opens that dock's settings, Quit closes it
                   ({More than one dock|moredocks})

-- Programs get into Hidden programs by being dragged off the dock while
-- they run ({Running programs|running}); you never type a name there.
@end

@node advshell "Shell drops and Keys"
*Shell drops* - see {Shell drops|shelldrops}
  Icons into a     Type paths, or Off (SHELLDROP)
  Shell
  Unpack archives  the Shell's drawer, RAM:, or the Unpack drawer
  to               (UNPACKTO)
  Unpack drawer    that drawer; the *...* button beside it opens a
                   drawer requester (UNPACKDIR)

*Keys* - see {Keyboard shortcuts|keys}
  Minimise window  CTRL ALT M by default (MINKEY)
  Minimise all     CTRL ALT SHIFT M (MINALLKEY)
  Auto-hide on/off CTRL ALT H (AHKEY)

-- Type a key combination, or NONE to switch one off. Modifiers alone
-- (CTRL ALT) are only allowed for the Hotkey.
@end

@node system "System"
  Font             name/size for the labels; empty = the screen's (FONT)
  Screen           a public screen; empty = Workbench (PUBSCREEN)
  Start delay      wait this many 1/50 s before opening (DELAY)
  Messages         Normal; Quiet (no requesters at all); Verbose (also
                   what is normally silent) (QUIET, VERBOSE)
  Beep             On or Off: the beep on a refused action (NOBEEP)
  Menu             Dock menu: a Dock title in the Workbench menu bar;
                   In Tools menu: four SimpleDock items under Tools
                   instead, as on 3.1.4 - for scripts that manage
                   Workbench menus by ARexx (TOOLSMENU)
  Crash reports    off by default; see {Crash reports|crash} (CRASHLOG)
  No border        no thin border around the dock (NOBORDER)
@end

@node buttons "Settings buttons"
  *Undo changes*     Restore the settings from when the window opened.
  *Load...*          Load settings and icons from another file.
  *Save as...*       Save a copy of your settings and icons to a file,
                     with an icon that starts a dock from it
                     ({More than one dock|moredocks}).
  *Advanced >>*      Show more settings; *<< Simple* hides them.

Numbers and text take effect on *Return* or *Tab*. A value that is not
valid, or one that would push icons off the dock, is refused with a beep
and the old value comes back.

-- Load and Save as do not change the dock's startup settings file.
-- The default is ENVARC:SimpleDock.config; see {More than one dock|moredocks}
-- to start a dock with a different file.
@end

@node shellicons "Shell icons and projects"
Icons set to *start from a Shell* (the CLI tooltype), *ARexx icons*
(REXX) and programs with *no icon of their own* are handed to Workbench,
so they start as a double-click does.

With "Shell icons: Ask first" ({Programs|programs}), the dock shows an
"Execute a file" requester first - not for icons with DONOTPROMPT.

A *project's default tool* is found as Workbench finds it: in the
project's drawer, then in Workbench's path, then in C:.
@end

@node moredocks "More than one dock"
You can run two docks at once, each with its own icons and settings -
say your programs along the bottom and your drawers along the right.

Open the settings window and choose *Save as...*, giving the new file
a name of your own, say Drawers.config. The file is written with a
project icon whose default tool is SimpleDock. Double-click that icon: a
second SimpleDock starts from the file and opens its own dock. Set its
edge and icons in its own settings window; they are saved to its file.
Its ARexx port is the file's name as spelled, Drawers.config, so scripts
can address each dock on its own (in quotes: address 'Drawers.config').

The first dock (the one named SIMPLEDOCK) is the overview: it shows the
programs that are not docked and the Workbench icon. Every further dock
starts as a plain launcher for its own icons - Other programs and
Workbench icon are set to Hide in its Advanced window, where you can
change them.

A further dock adds nothing to the Workbench menus. To reach it, open
the main dock's settings window: Advanced, Programs, *Other docks* lists
the docks running. Pick one, then *Settings...* opens that dock's own
settings window and *Quit* closes it. (A dock started while no main
dock is running keeps a Dock menu of its own.)

A dock that opens where another dock already is moves beside it on the
same edge, or to a free edge, and keeps that place. The dock that was
there first never moves.

To start it with Workbench, move the file and its icon to SYS:WBStartup.
From a Shell or a script: SimpleDock CONFIG=<file> - while a dock is
already running, this one takes the file's name as its port.

A dock can also be made from a *drawer of icons* (CONFIG set to the
drawer): its settings live in its icon's tooltypes.
@end

@node configfile "The settings file"
*ENVARC:SimpleDock.config* is plain text: settings lines, then one line
per icon. A keyword in parentheses is disabled.

    ; SimpleDock settings
    POS=B
    HOVERTEXT
    (AUTOHIDE=3)
    ; icons
    SYS:Utilities/MultiView
    LABEL Viewer
    SYS:System/Shell

*LABEL* renames the icon before it; *IMAGE* <path> borrows another
icon's picture.

The same keywords work as *tooltypes* of the SimpleDock icon and on the
*command line*: tooltypes, then the file, then the command line, each
overriding the one before. The keywords: {Keywords|keywords}.
@end

@node keywords "Keywords"
Settings file, tooltypes and command line alike.

  CONFIG=<file>       settings file (default ENVARC:SimpleDock.config)
  POS=<edge><align>   T, B, L or R, then C, S or E to align (POS=BS)
  DISTANCE=<n>        gap to the screen edge, pixels (default 4)
  OFFSET=<n>          shift along the edge, pixels
  ICONDIST=<n>        space between icons (default 2)
  SCALEICONW=<n>      shrink icons wider than this (0 = own size)
  SCALEICONH=<n>      shrink icons taller than this
  LIMIT=<n>           at most n icons (default: as many as fit)
  RADIUS=<n>          corner radius (default 12)
  NOTEXT              no labels
  HOVERTEXT           a label only under the pointer
  NOICON              labels only, no icons
  NOBORDER            no thin border
  FONT=<name/size>    label font
  TINT=#rrggbb        colour (default #383838)
  TRANSPARENT         tinted, see-through background
  OPACITY=<n>         its darkness, 0-100
  CUSTOMTINT=#rrggbb  the custom colour in the settings window's list
  AUTOHIDE=<n>        0 off, 1 instant; otherwise n x 70 ms per slide
  ONTOP               stay in front of other windows
  HOTKEY=<keys>       the hotkey (default ctrl alt shift); mbutton or
                      ctrl mbutton...: the middle mouse button
  MINKEY=<keys>       minimise the active window (default ctrl alt m)
  MINALLKEY=<keys>    minimise all (default ctrl alt shift m)
  AHKEY=<keys>        auto-hide on/off (default ctrl alt h); none = off
  RUNNING=<n>         0 off, 1 dot, 2 dot and number (default 2)
  CLICKNEW            a click starts a new copy; Shift+click: to front
  WBSTART             Workbench starts the programs
  SHELLASK            Shell icons ask first
  DROPMOVE            drops onto a drawer move on the same disk
  HOLDTIME=<n>        long press, tenths of a second (default 3)
  RUNHIDE             programs not on the dock are not shown
  IGNORE=<names>      hide these running programs ("Name,Name")
  WBHIDE              no Workbench icon
  SHELLDROP=<n>       1 icons dropped onto a Shell are typed (default),
                      0 off
  UNPACKTO=<n>        archives unpack: 0 the Shell's drawer, 1 RAM:,
                      2 UNPACKDIR
  UNPACKDIR=<drawer>  the drawer for UNPACKTO=2
  PUBSCREEN=<name>    the public screen to open on
  DELAY=<n>           wait n/50 s before opening
  QUIET / VERBOSE     suppress requesters / show extra messages
  NOBEEP              no beep on a refused action
  TOOLSMENU           the Dock menu's items under Tools, no Dock title
  CRASHLOG            crash reports ({Crash reports|crash})
@end

@node arexx "ARexx"
The port is *SIMPLEDOCK* (a second dock: its file's name). Every command
returns 0 = success, 5 = busy, or 10 = failure. While a dock icon is
being dragged, most commands that change the dock return 5; retry after the
drag. GETPOS, LIST and GET remain available.

-> marks a RESULT (enable with OPTIONS RESULTS). Identify an icon by its
label, file name or path, or #n for its position (#1 is first). Quote
arguments that contain spaces.

== The dock
  SHOW / HIDE             open the dock again / close it (the dock
                          keeps running)
  REVEAL                  as the hotkey
  REFRESH                 reopen the dock
  GETPOS               -> "x y w h" of the dock, then of the icon
                          clicked last
  QUIT                    close the dock and exit

== Icons
  LIST [NAMES]         -> the icons' paths (or labels), one per line
  ADD <path> [AT n | AFTER icon | BEFORE icon] [LABEL text]
  REMOVE <icon>
  MOVE <icon> AT n | AFTER icon | BEFORE icon

== Settings
  SET <keyword> ...       as the settings file; parentheses disable;
                          quote values with spaces
  GET [<keyword>]      -> its value (1 / 0 for a switch; empty: not
                          set); without a keyword: every setting
  SAVE [<file>]           save settings and icons to <file>, or to the
                          dock's own file if no file is given
  RELOAD                  the dock's own file again
  LOAD <file>             another file's settings and icons
  SETTINGS [ADVANCED]     the settings window

== For bug reports
  DROPINFO / HOTKEYINFO -> what the dock sees of Shell windows / the
                          hotkey
  BENCH                -> drawing times on this machine

== Examples
    address SIMPLEDOCK 'ADD "RAM:" AFTER Shell LABEL "Ram Disk"'
    address SIMPLEDOCK 'SET AUTOHIDE=3 RADIUS=0'
    address SIMPLEDOCK 'SAVE'
    options results
    address SIMPLEDOCK 'GET AUTOHIDE'; say result
@end

@node limits "Known limitations"
== Running programs
  AmigaOS keeps no list of running programs: the dock knows them by
  their windows and names. A program with no window, a renamed task or
  one started by a script is not seen; a click then starts a copy.
  A program the dock did not start is noticed when one of its windows
  becomes active, or within 2 seconds.
  Shell windows are found by asking each Shell's console; a console
  that does not answer gives a count but no window list.
  Minimise needs OS 3.2, or layers.library 45 (3.1.4, 3.9): the dock
  then hides the window's layers itself, and a program that brings its
  own window forward may show it again. Quitting the dock shows every
  minimised window.
  Close sends what the window's close gadget sends; a program may ask
  first, and a window without a close gadget cannot be closed (beep).
  Programs the dock started keep running when it quits; a few hundred
  bytes for each stay in memory until a reboot.
  Icons dropped straight into a KingCON window may not reach the dock
  (KingCON handles them itself); the dock's Shell icon does the job.

== The dock
  One row of icons. When it is full, the last place lists the rest.
  Windows cannot be round or see-through, so the corners and the
  transparent look use a picture of what is behind the dock. When that
  changes, the dock steps aside for a moment to take a new one.
  With auto-hide, icons cannot be dropped onto the hidden dock:
  Workbench locks the screen during a drag. Press the hotkey first.
  A hover label over a Shell window can leave a faint box behind.
  In front gives up after three tries in a row with nothing pressed in
  between (another window that keeps itself in front would fight it
  for ever) and rests while the settings window is open.
  Before changing screen mode, quit the dock (or close its window with
  ARexx HIDE) and start it again afterwards (or ARexx SHOW).

== Older systems
  Without ReAction's chooser.gadget (before 3.2) the settings lists
  are cycle gadgets: a click steps to the next choice.
  Marking a whole text field with a double-click needs 3.2.
  On 3.5 the Dock menu is in the Tools menu.

== Settings
  Version 1.0 does not know the settings new in 1.1 and shows them as
  icons marked "?". Remove those lines if you go back.
@end

@node crash "Crash reports"
If SimpleDock crashes, switch *Crash reports* on ({System|system}) and
repeat what you did. On the next crash, SimpleDock writes
*ENVARC:SimpleDock-crash.txt*, shows a message and closes. The report
records the error, its location in the program, and your system and
settings. Attach it to your {bug report|bugs}.

-- Crash reports cost nothing while nothing goes wrong, and catch only
-- errors in the dock's own task; if the whole system is unstable, reboot.
@end

@node bugs "Bug reports"
Report bugs at: https://github.com/renewable-intel/SimpleDock/issues

Include:

  - Steps to reproduce the problem.
  - What you expected and what actually happened.
  - Your SimpleDock and AmigaOS versions, CPU, graphics card or AGA,
    and screen mode.
  - A {crash report|crash}, if one was created.

For a Shell-drop problem, include the output of:

    rx "options results; address SIMPLEDOCK 'DROPINFO'; say result"

For a hotkey problem, replace DROPINFO with HOTKEYINFO.
@end
